## About

I am a Ph.D. student supervised by [Xiaoxiao Li](https://tea.ece.ubc.ca/) at the [Trusted and Efficient AI (TEA) Lab](https://tea.ece.ubc.ca/), [University of British Columbia](https://www.ubc.ca/).

From 2022 to 2023, I was a research assistant supervised by [Xiaodan Liang](https://lemondan.github.io/) at the [Human Cyber Physical Intelligence Integration (HCP) Lab](https://www.sysu-hcp.net/), [Sun Yat-Sen University](https://www.sysu.edu.cn/index.htm), Guangzhou, China. I received my master's degree in Pattern Recognition and Intelligent Systems from [South China University of Technology](https://www.scut.edu.cn/en/main.htm). During my master's studies, I was co-advised by [Zhuliang Yu](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=oAUB9cQAAAAJ&hl=en) and [Wei Wu](https://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=zh-TW&user=HKmFkdwAAAAJ) at the Center for Brain-Computer Interfaces and Information Processing.

My research interests lie in Bayesian learning and machine learning methods for interdisciplinary applications. I am particularly interested in the intersection of deep learning and Bayesian statistics, as well as generative models, explainability, and graph representation learning.

## Research Focus

- **Evidence-grounded visual reasoning:** benchmarks and reusable evidence representations for reasoning over ultra-resolution images and whole-slide pathology images.
- **Multimodal computational pathology:** combining heterogeneous foundation models, biological knowledge, and image features for mutation prediction and survival analysis.
- **Controllable medical image generation:** diffusion models for interactive tumor editing and identity-preserving brain aging modeling.
